Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a session and how do we meet?

Each session is strictly 15 minutes. Once confirmed via OTP, you'll receive a one-click web link (or WhatsApp/Zoom/SMS call link depending on your preference). No special app downloads required!

Can parents book sessions on behalf of their children?

Yes! Simply select "A Parent" in the booking form. We'll collect your contact information for billing & updates, and ask for the student's name for the coach to personalize the session.

What packages do you offer?

We offer 4 flexible coaching packages: 1 Session (₹300), 5 Sessions (₹1,299), 10 Sessions (₹2,799), and 15 Sessions (₹4,299). All packages include 1-on-1 personalized micro-coaching.

Do I need to turn my camera on?

Camera is 100% optional! You can do voice-only, audio, or text chat if that feels more comfortable for you.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

You can reschedule or cancel at any time with a single tap from the SMS/email confirmation link.
