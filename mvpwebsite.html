Between school deadlines, board exams, and the constant pull of your phone, it is easy to feel overwhelmed. But carrying it all in silence makes it heavier than it needs to be.

The Remainder

Talking to someone helps.

Whether it's a friend who gets it, a supportive teacher, a counselor, or a parent—sharing the mental load cuts the stress in half. You don't have to figure it all out by yourself tonight.

01. Pause the Ping

Put your phone in another room or turn on Do Not Disturb when tackling assignments. 25 minutes of focus beats 3 hours of distracted multitasking.

02. Share the Weight

Stuck on a project or drowning in work? Tell someone. Admitting you're stressed isn't weakness; it's the first step to clearing your head.

03. Close the Tabs

Late-night cramming hurts tomorrow's focus. Give yourself permission to log off, sleep, and reset for a fresh start in the morning.
